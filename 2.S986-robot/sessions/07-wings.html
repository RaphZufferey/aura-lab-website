Module 7 — Wing fabrication

Summary

Both wings: carbon skeleton, membrane cut to the geometry chosen in Module 6, bonded and stitched at the load-bearing points.

Requirements

Parts Carbon rods and strips, wing roots, 2× nylon adapters per wing, ripstop nylon membrane, 3M tape, superglue, thread
Tools Cutting mat, steel rule, scalpel, needle, printed full-size template of the chosen geometry

Wing roots

The wing roots are carbon, cut to mate with the gears from Module 5. A champfer needs to be cut in the wing roots for the countersunk head of the M3 screw.

Example and tips for building the wing

The whole wing is built over the printed pattern, working from the leading edge outwards. Everything is cut oversize and trimmed to the outline at the very end - margin is easy to remove and impossible to add back.

  1. Decide on the shape and print the pattern on paper. Print it 1:1 - check the scale before cutting anything, by measuring a known dimension on the printout against a rule. Printer drivers scale to fit by default, and a pattern that came out at 97% quietly discards the geometry chosen in Module 6.

  2. Cut two membranes with a 3 cm margin all round the pattern. Use the ripstop nylon listed in Parts.

  3. Bond the leading edge. Lay a strip of 3M tape along the leading-edge region of the membrane, press the leading-edge rod down onto the tape, and wrap the membrane around the rod. This joint carries the highest load of any on the wing — everything downstream pulls against it.

  4. Lay out the carbon strips. Cut each to approximate length plus 3 cm, position them on the membrane over the pattern, and bond them with superglue.

  5. Stitch the strips down, at minimum at every intersection point and at the extremities. Superglue alone will peel off the membrane under repeated flapping; the stitches are what keep the skeleton attached once the glue starts to fail.

  6. Trim the excess beyond the outline with a blade, cutting on the mat.

Repeat for the second wing, and remember that it is flipped.

Assembly

  1. Mount each wing in its wing root using the two nylon adapters.
  2. Mount the wing roots in the robot, onto the gears from Module 5.
WarningBoth wings must match

Asymmetry between left and right shows up as a roll bias that may not be possible to correct later. See Module 11 for trimming out. Weigh both wings and compare spar positions against the template before bonding.
