Labspace and Equipment

Test Tank

Test Tank

Our lab test tank allows for in water experimentation with our robotic platforms as well as experiments to study the transition between air and water for our aerial aquatic systems. The tank uses a UV filter to for cleaning meaning no chlorine or salt is needed. It is approximately 12m x 5m x 1.5m and has rails for towing tank experiments in the future.

Windshape

Windshape

Image Source

We have access to a windshape fan array for flight characterization and testing. It can produce windspeeds of upwards of 15m/s and simulate complex flows with different windspeeds for different fan sections.
